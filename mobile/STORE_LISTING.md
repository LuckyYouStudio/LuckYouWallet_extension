## Review notes (App Review Information → Notes)

```
LuckYou Wallet is a non-custodial cryptocurrency wallet.

- Users create or import their own wallet; the recovery phrase and private
  keys are stored only in the iOS Keychain on the device. The developer has
  no servers, no user accounts, and no access to user funds.
- The app does not offer exchange, trading, swaps, ICOs, mining, or fiat
  on/off-ramp services. It only shows balances and lets users sign and
  broadcast their own transfers via public blockchain nodes.
- No sign-in is required. To test: tap "Create wallet", write down the 12
  words, pick the 3 requested words on the verification screen, set any
  password, and you will reach the home screen. Use the network switcher at
  the top to see Ethereum, Bitcoin, Solana and Tron accounts.
- The app is not available in China mainland.
```
